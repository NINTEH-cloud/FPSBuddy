# FPS Buddy

FPS meter and advisor for Guild Wars 2, as a [Raidcore Nexus](https://raidcore.gg/Nexus) addon. The goal: the best-looking game at the FPS you want.

## What's inside

- **FPS meter on screen** – FPS, frame time, 1% low and a small graph. Colours show whether you reach your target FPS. Can show only when FPS drops.
- **Stutter log** – every hitch with time and map, plus how much memory and processor GW2 uses.
- **Advice for your graphics settings** – FPS Buddy reads your GW2 graphics settings (read-only) and compares them with your target FPS:
  - below your target: what gains the most FPS for the least visual loss (Character Model Limit, Effect LOD, Render Sampling, Shadows, ...);
  - well above your target: what can look nicer at little cost (Textures, Shaders, Antialiasing, ...).
- **Why is my FPS lower?** – the main causes in one list: your settings, Windows (power plan, Game Mode, background recording, free memory, laptop on battery) and your addons.
- **Addon cost** – how much all Nexus addons together cost per frame, and a measure-and-compare tool to find the heaviest addon.

The addon is available in Dutch, English and German.

## Installation

1. Install [Raidcore Nexus](https://raidcore.gg/Nexus).
2. Download `FpsBuddy.dll` from the [latest release](https://github.com/NINTEH-cloud/FPSBuddy/releases/latest).
3. Put it in `Guild Wars 2/addons/` and load it in the Nexus addon library.
4. Open the window with **ALT+SHIFT+F**.

Updates arrive automatically through Nexus.

## Reporting a bug or idea

Open an [issue](https://github.com/NINTEH-cloud/FPSBuddy/issues), or use the **Report a bug** button in the addon's settings.

## Fair play

FPS Buddy only measures and reads. It never changes the game or your settings, and does not read or change game memory.
